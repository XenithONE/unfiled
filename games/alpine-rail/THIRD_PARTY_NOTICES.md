# Third-party materials

- Three.js — MIT. https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js
- Anton, Inter and IBM Plex Mono — SIL Open Font License; distributed via Fontsource.
- Rocky Terrain, Amal Kumar / Poly Haven — CC0. https://polyhaven.com/a/rocky_terrain
- Snow 02, Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven — CC0. https://polyhaven.com/a/snow_02

The two Poly Haven textures are the same files EVEREST bundles (`games/everest/assets/rock-color.jpg`, `snow-color.jpg`); ALPINE LINE imports them from there rather than keeping copies. Everything else — grass, gravel, stone, tunnel rock, trees, buildings, the train, signs and all sound — is generated in code. The railway company "Viafier Alpina", its livery and every station name are fictional.
